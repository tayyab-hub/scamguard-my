# Risk register

Table 12 uses an explicit qualitative scale: likelihood 1 unlikely, 2 plausible, 3 expected/exposed; impact 1 minor, 2 moderate, 3 material. Risk level is likelihood × impact: 1–2 Low, 3–4 Medium, 6–9 High. Ratings are engineering judgement for this capstone, not measured incident probabilities. Residual risk describes the remaining concern after current controls; a tested control does not make the risk disappear. The project owner is responsible for manual follow-up.
