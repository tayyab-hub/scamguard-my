# Claim audit and corrected meanings

The audit searched report-support Markdown and root project-state documents for all eleven requested expressions: “detects scams”, “prevents scams”, “accurate”, “safe”, “secure”, “AI powered”, “real time”, “forensic”, “guaranteed”, “verified” and “trusted”, including common case/hyphen variants. It also checked the final Word text. The occurrence register records each match, location, context, disposition and evidence pointer: [claim-audit.csv](evidence/claim-audit.csv). This is an occurrence audit, not a claim that a word filter can establish scientific validity.

| Claim attacked | Defensible final meaning / correction | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Detects/prevents scams” or loss reduction | Decision support assesses available indicators. No measured prevention, loss reduction, causal outcome or universal scam verdict is claimed. | FINAL_PROBLEM_STATEMENT.md; FINAL_CONCLUSION.md; LIMITATIONS.md |
| “Accurate” | Exact classifier metrics are reported with test denominators, grouped-split method, minority errors and bias. They do not measure fused risk, all four modes or population effectiveness. | MODEL_EVALUATION.md; URL_MODEL_EVALUATION.md; FINAL_EVALUATION.md |
| “Safe” | Low/valid/decode/CRC never establishes identity, legitimacy or a risk-free action. Technical “safe error” wording in current engineering docs is replaced by generic/bounded error behaviour. “Safe Browsing” remains the product's proper name. | Privacy/method docs; generic-error and QR tests; official Google references |
| “Secure” | Cookie Secure is a concrete attribute, and secure context is a browser term. Overall claims are decomposed into Argon2id, opaque sessions, CSRF/origin, ownership and bounded-input controls; no certification or complete pentest is claimed. | AUTHENTICATION.md; SECURITY_REVIEW.md; OWASP references |
| “AI powered” | The implemented learned components are Message Logistic Regression and URL random forest. Phone/QR/payment/rules are deterministic. Optional external AI is disabled by default and in evaluation. | SOLUTION_APPROACH.md; EXAMINER_QA.md |
| “Real time” | Live camera describes local frame detection, not a measured production latency guarantee. No live threat/reputation feed is implemented. | “Screenshots prove production or human success” | Nine actual local captures retain Chrome/viewport/time/fixture/SHA metadata; three are embedded as Figures 9–11. No physical camera, volunteer or production authenticated claim. | report-screenshots.json; FINAL_FIGURES.md |

Historical task documents keep their original results and dates under explicit supersession banners; they are not rewritten to pretend later features existed earlier. Rubric descriptors and quoted examples of bad claims are labelled as such. Acceptance instructions, prospective work and source titles are not present-tense application-result claims.

No identified unsupported affirmative outcome claim remains in the prepared report/defence content. This conclusion is limited to the reviewed wording and sources. Missing original dates, human outcomes and independent validation remain visible in the report and owner checklist; they have not been converted into passing evidence.
