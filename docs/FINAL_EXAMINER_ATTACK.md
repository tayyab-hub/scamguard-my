# Three-pass examiner deduction attack

Classification: **A FIXED**, **B USER ACTION REQUIRED**, **C INHERENT LIMITATION**, **D EXAMINER SUBJECTIVITY**. Severity refers to a plausible academic deduction, not a measured security vulnerability. “Fixed” means the specified document/evidence defect has been corrected; it does not turn an unperformed test into a pass. Official criteria are the supplied proposal rubrics, Excellent 9–10.

## Full-mark gate and remaining reasons

Practical document corrections have been carried out; the remaining high/medium owner gaps are deliberately **not counted as resolved**. Planning and Report Format currently score below Excellent. Live Knowledge and Delivery cannot be certified from prepared material. No claim of “70/70 ready” or “40/40 live ready” is made.

Every identified remaining report deduction reason: incomplete original duration; original approved deadlines/non-Git chronology absent; blank required cover fields; unknown applicability of proposal rules to the final submission, page limit and declarations; possible requirement to retain original approved objectives; examiner judgement of depth/style; and any unsupported claims the student adds later. The project's inherent dataset, phone/payment, security and operational limits are disclosed. They do not automatically deduct marks under an unlisted criterion, but they would defeat claims exceeding the evidence.

Every identified remaining presentation deduction reason: magnitude of potential impact not demonstrated empirically; presenter may misunderstand or overstate methods, metrics, privacy or release evidence; mock defence unperformed; actual timing unmeasured; final room/device/network behaviour untested; slide reading, weak projection or eye contact; unfamiliar follow-up questions; and subjective judgements of significance/elaboration. A prepared script is evidence of preparation, not live performance.

## Ten most likely deductions

1. **Missing deadlines in the Gantt:** still open; supply approved planned/actual dates (B HIGH).
2. **Blank cover or wrong final submission format:** still open; fill personal fields and confirm institution rules (B HIGH).
3. **Cannot explain model versus rules:** defence pack corrects the material; demonstrate understanding in hostile rehearsal (A/B HIGH).
4. **Accuracy claimed as real-world scam prevention:** corrected to held-out classifier results, minority recall and bias; preserve those limits orally (A/C).
5. **Weak related-system comparison:** corrected with three critical primary-source reviews, including Truecaller URL and VirusTotal private-scanning distinctions (A).
6. **Phone/QR “valid means safe”:** corrected; practise identity versus metadata and CRC versus legitimacy (A/C).
7. **Demo fails on camera/network/email:** upload/offline fallback prepared; execute actual device/inbox rehearsal (A/B medium).
8. **Private data isolation asserted only from UI:** all-mode local API/DB evidence linked; owner records controlled production A/B checks (A/B medium).
9. **Stale release version or unsupported test counts:** final merge/provider identity and separate machine evidence reconciled; do not treat later documentation as a new deploy (A).
10. **Over-time, quiet or read-out presentation:** structured 13:15 plan and delivery protocol ready; four rehearsals and audience feedback remain essential (B HIGH).
