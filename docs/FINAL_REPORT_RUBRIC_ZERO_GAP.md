**Strict report self-mark: 65/70.** Scores below 10 are tied to the specific missing evidence above. Missing usability participants or a penetration test is not silently added as an eighth official criterion. Such evidence matters only where an effectiveness claim is made or the assessor separately requires it.

An examiner could still refuse 70/70 because of incomplete original duration/deadlines, blank cover and unknown final submission rules, a different interpretation of retrospective objectives under a proposal rubric, or their judgement of the depth and writing.
